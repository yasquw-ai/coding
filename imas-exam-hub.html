Section 03

Exam Rules & Regulations

Please go through these rules with your child so they know what is expected of them before, during and after every paper.

Part 1

Code of Conduct

  1. Students are expected to be present at the examination hall at least 30 minutes before the start of the examination.
  2. Students should observe silence at ALL times during the examination.
  3. The teacher-invigilator reserves the right to expel any student from the exam hall if he/she misbehaves during the examination, after consultation with the level supervisors.
  4. Unauthorised materials are NOT allowed.
  5. No food or drinks are allowed. Only water bottles without labels are allowed.
  6. Any student who attempts to cheat will be dealt with severely.
  7. Students sitting for the examination must adhere to the school rules with regard to attire, hairstyle and accessories.
Part 2

Before the Exam

  1. All students must be present and seated at their designated desks before the starting time.
  2. Authorised examination tools and equipment (pens, pencils, erasers, etc.) are to be set out on the desks. It is recommended to have extra pens and pencils.
  3. All books, notebooks and scraps of paper should be removed from the desks and placed inside students’ bags. All bags are to be placed neatly in a row at the back of the classroom.
  4. Students are to check their pockets to ensure that there are no scraps of paper, notes or books in their possession.
  5. Electronic gadgets and communication devices, such as (but not limited to) mobile phones, are NOT allowed.
  6. Students should use the washroom before arriving for their exam, as they will not be permitted to leave during the first half of the exam.
  7. No extra time will be given to latecomers, even with an excuse. Students who report 30 minutes after the exam has started will not be allowed to sit for the paper.
  8. While exam papers are being distributed, students are to wait for the invigilator’s permission to start the exam all together.
  9. Listen to the invigilator’s instructions carefully before the exam, and read the exam paper instructions immediately upon receiving the question paper.
  10. Students are to ensure they have been given the correct paper and the correct number of pages.
Part 3

During the Exam

  1. Do not communicate with other students. If there are queries, students are to raise their hands and clarify with the invigilator.
  2. Students are not permitted to share any materials during the examination.
  3. Students should ensure that their exam sheets and answers cannot be read by other students.
  4. Students who are caught cheating or attempting to cheat will be dealt with severely.
  5. Students need to ensure all sheets and questions are answered and checked before they end their exam.
  6. Students must stop writing immediately when the invigilator announces the end of the examination.
Part 4

After the Exam

  1. At the end of each paper, remain quiet and seated. Ensure that all exam papers are handed in as instructed by the invigilator.
  2. No student is allowed to take any writing paper, used or unused, out of the examination room.
  3. No one is allowed to leave the examination hall before the end of the time allotted for the paper.
  4. Before leaving the exam hall, ensure that your desk is clean and in line with the desk in front of you. Push the chair back under the desk.
  5. Leave the examination hall quickly and quietly. Remember to take all your belongings with you.
Section 04

Exam Attendance Policy

Regular, punctual attendance makes sure every student has a fair chance to sit each paper. Please read this policy carefully.

Arrive before8:00 AM
Exams start8:20 AM
Dismissal on exam days11:15 AM
Part 1

Attendance During Examinations

  1. Students sitting for the exam must adhere to the school rules with regard to attire, hairstyle and accessories.
  2. Students are to attend school as normal, before 8:00 AM. Exams will start at 8:20 AM.
  3. Students who report to school 30 minutes after the paper has started will NOT be allowed to sit for the paper.
  4. Students will be dismissed at 11:15 AM on exam days.
Part 2

Absence & Medical Leave

  1. In case of illness requiring medical leave, students must provide an official medical certificate. Upon returning to school, students must report to their respective class teacher with the certificate.
  2. Students with a valid medical certificate or pre-approved leave will be scheduled for supplementary exams.
Important

Any student absent from an exam without an official medical certificate or pre-approved leave will receive a zero. Notes from parents or guardians will not be accepted as valid excuses.

Failure to attend the supplementary exam will result in a zero mark for that paper.
